# 2026-09-24 — `36d3b559`: installed login, twice

EFI `36d3b5595d67` (`cursor/m8-grubcfg-esp-8366`). Lease `10.99.99.148/24`
on every boot of this sha. Front USB 2 UDisk. Toshiba `0480:a004` on xHCI
p11. `usbsoak.txt` stayed off.

## What the old disk was

The first product boot of this EFI, still on the partial install, printed
`grubcfg=no`, staged `BOOTX64.EFI bytes=139264`, and stopped at GRUB 2.12
`grub>`. Ubuntu read of that volume: no `/boot/grub/grub.cfg`, 279 modules
under `/boot/grub/x86_64-efi`, and `grep -c "Minimal BASH-like"` of
`BOOTX64.EFI` was 0. The prompt was a missing menu file, not a USB miss.
That GPT was zeroed (LBA 0–33 and the 8 GiB backup GPT only).

## Blank-disk install

Empty `raynuf.txt` was removed. SPA Start (`POST /vms/1/spec/2/2048/8192/1/linux_iso`
then `POST /vms/1/start`) on blank media printed `image=ISO-BOOTX64`,
`[vda] 16777216`, auto-answer `setup-disk`, `RAYNU-V-M7-ISO-INSTALL-OK`,
`Installation finished. No error reported.`, and
`Installation is complete. Please reboot.` The live installer then sat at
`localhost:~#`. Force Off. Ubuntu `lsblk` showed the Toshiba 48M vfat + 8G
ext4. Empty `raynuf.txt` was put back (`sudo touch`).

## What it means

The installed 8 GiB slice survived two hypervisor Force Offs on one EFI and
came back to the same login. That is the repeat A2 was waiting on. It is
not PERC, not the whole Toshiba (`bytes=320072933376` stays outside the
virtio window), and not A4s: no browser was opened on these persist boots.
Do not reflash. Do not curl Start. Do not run `setup-disk`.
