What this page does not claim

  • A letter is not a purchase order, not 1.0, not cluster, not Windows. Cluster is M9 and the K rows; Windows is B13.
  • A row becomes lived only with R640 evidence (a commit SHA, a COM2 marker, an evidence document). Latitude, nested QEMU, host tests and CI do not make a row lived.
  • Counts are not a score. There is no percent-ready and no months-to-letter here on purpose; the last one drifted from the catalog within a week.
  • Everest remains closed at 99% on its own page. Nothing here reopens it.
  • Rollback remains GitHub Latest v0.1.0-everest-closed. Do not format UBUNTU0. Do not partition RAYNU-SPARE.
